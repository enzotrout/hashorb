# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

HashOrb is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive participation include:

- being respectful and constructive in technical discussions
- giving and accepting feedback in good faith
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people
- helping keep discussions useful, factual, and relevant
- respecting differences in experience, background, and perspective

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or demeaning language
- deliberate disruption of project discussions
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- repeated unwanted contact or attention
- other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in HashOrb project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other official project communication channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately rather than escalating the situation publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and discreetly as practical.
